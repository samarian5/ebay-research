What this board measures

  • Active market only — New + Buy It Now, logged out. Sold search and auctions are out of scope.
  • Units come from eBay's 63+ sold badges, which are a floor, not an exact count.
  • units/day needs two laps: it is the badge gain between runs divided by elapsed days.
  • Volume gate — a keyword with fewer than 10 badged listings is marked unmeasured and is never ranked.
  • Attrition — share of last lap's listings that vanished from this lap. High attrition plus low units means the niche churns.
